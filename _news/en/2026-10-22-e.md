MetaboBank Search services will be unavailable due to the system maintenance to strengthen security against for multiple critical vulnerabilities.
During the service suspension, please use [DDBJ Search](https://ddbj.nig.ac.jp/search) for MetaboBank ID-based searches.  
We apologize for the inconvenience caused.
