To submit human data obtained in research subject to the [Ethical Guidelines for Medical and Biological Research Involving Human Subjects](/policies-e.html#submission-of-human-data) to BioData Science Initiative (BSI) databases, you must first [apply to DBCLS for data submission](https://humandbs.dbcls.jp/en/data-submission) and obtain approval.

Due to an increase in the number of applications, approval is currently taking more than two months, and incomplete applications take even longer. If you are planning to submit human data, please apply for data submission as early as possible.

- [Submission of human data](/policies-e.html#submission-of-human-data)
- [Application to DBCLS for data provision](https://humandbs.dbcls.jp/en/data-submission)
