The [INSDC](https://www.insdc.org/) has developed a set of Minimal specifications to ensure the consistency, interoperability, and reliability (FAIR) of nucleotide sequence data shared across all member databases. [INSDC Minimal Specifications](/insdc/insdc-min-spec-e.html)

We are pleased to announce that a paper describing the INSDC Minimal Specifications initiative was published on 7 September 2026.

Yuichi Kodama, Colman O’Cathail, Linda Yankie, the INSDC Implementation Committee, The INSDC specifications—foundations for a FAIR and global INSDC, Database, Volume 2026, 2026, baag054, [https://doi.org/10.1093/database/baag054](https://doi.org/10.1093/database/baag054)
