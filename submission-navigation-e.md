If you are unsure which database to submit your data to or how to submit it, please use the [BSI Submission Navigator](https://bsi.nig.ac.jp/submit). Simply select a few items about your data, and it will guide you to the appropriate database and explain how to submit your data. It is also recommended for first-time submitters.
